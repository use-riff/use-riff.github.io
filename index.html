a different route for launch fees.

One continuous signal path from creator to artist to charity — not four separate features.

01 — select

select

A coin creator selects an artist they want to support.

02 — reserve

reserve

A defined share of platform fees is reserved for that artist.

03 — claim

claim

The artist verifies their identity and chooses whether to accept.

04 — redirect

redirect

Declined or expired allocations go to an independent music charity.

Selection does not mean affiliation. Until an artist accepts, the artist has not approved or endorsed the coin.
